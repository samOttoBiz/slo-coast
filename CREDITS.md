## Opening illustration

`assets/coast-illustration.webp` is a 1536 × 1024 generated illustration, 313,988 bytes. The original provenance records creation September 11, 2026 with the built-in Imagegen tool, followed by local WebP encoding at quality 82. It depicts an imaginary Central California-inspired coastline, not a real named beach, map, destination photograph, measured sun path or conditions report. It remains visibly labelled in the hero. Destination cards do not reuse this image.

No third-party font is shipped. Existing SVG symbols are interface icons, not documentary scenery.
